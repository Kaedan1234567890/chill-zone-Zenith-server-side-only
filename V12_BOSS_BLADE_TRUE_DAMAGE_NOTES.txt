CHILL ZONE ZENITH 0.12.1-alpha - Boss Blade True Damage (server-side Polymer source)

Applied to the correct uploaded server-side Polymer conversion source.

Boss Blade active-ability rules:
- Ender Dragon Blade: 50% of target MAX health through armor/shields; no intentional armor durability damage.
- Ravager Blade: 50% of target MAX health through armor/shields; no intentional armor durability damage.
- Elder Guardian Blade: 50% of target MAX health through armor/shields; no intentional armor durability damage.
- Warden Blade: delayed beam deals 50% of target MAX health through armor/shields; no intentional armor durability damage.
- Wither Blade: 50% of target MAX health through armor/shields; removed extra Wither damage so the active hit remains the intended 50%.

Zenith:
- Zenith Storm remains lethal through armor/shields.
- Vanilla Totems are still allowed to save the target.
- A target is processed only once per Zenith Storm activation, preventing overlapping lanes from instantly killing them after a Totem pop.
- Equipped armor is intentionally chipped by about 10% of each piece's maximum durability, but the chip itself will not destroy the piece.

Implementation note:
- Armor/shield bypass uses the vanilla sonic-boom damage source rather than normal player-attack damage.
- Existing Polymer/server-side conversion, progression, crafting, unique-item locks, models, sounds, and other sword abilities were left intact.
