CHILL ZONE ZENITH 0.12.2-alpha - Armor Slot Build Fix

Fixes the Minecraft 26.2 compile error caused by LivingEntity#getArmorSlots() no longer being available in these mappings.

Zenith armor-chip logic now explicitly reads HEAD, CHEST, LEGS, and FEET with LivingEntity#getItemBySlot(EquipmentSlot).

No balance changes:
- Boss Blade active abilities: 50% max-health true damage through armor/shields, no intentional armor durability damage.
- Zenith: lethal true damage, Totems can trigger, ~10% durability chip on equipped armor.
